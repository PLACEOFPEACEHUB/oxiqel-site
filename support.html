Support, designed before it is needed

No OXIQEL product has shipped, so there is nothing to support yet. What exists is the workflow design, built alongside the order system so that support is never improvised after the first sale. Every rule below is a design commitment, published now so it can be held against us later.

Warranty

Keyed to your serial number

Delivery confirmation starts the warranty clock, and the serial number registered from the supplier's own event is the warranty key. No paper registration is required. The standard term in the draft policy is a one year limited warranty, with the legal floors of each market applied where the law requires more.

Returns

Thirty days from delivery

The draft return window is thirty days from delivery, checked against launch market law before anything goes live. A return starts in the account, produces a return authorisation and a label, and the refund goes to the original payment method, marked complete only when the payment processor confirms it.

Channels

Written first

Support runs written first, by email and chat, in three tiers: account and order questions, technical help, then supplier and warranty escalation. Every ticket links the order's full audit history, and support may never promise a status the audit log does not show.

One honest gap, stated plainly: whether a warranty claim ends in advance replacement or repair depends on the manufacturer's own warranty terms, which are not known until the quotation replies arrive. The workflow holds that as an open gate rather than inventing a promise.

Questions about the program today go through the contact page. The full draft terms live on the policies page.
