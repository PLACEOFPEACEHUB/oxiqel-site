Policies · Draft

Policies

DRAFT templates for legal review. This store is a development preview; nothing is for sale and these terms bind nobody. Final terms require a consumer and product lawyer in each launch market and the regulatory floors in the compliance checklist, such as the European two year legal guarantee and the fourteen day distance selling withdrawal right.

Terms of sale

Orders are offers to buy, accepted only when the manufacturer confirms production capacity. Built to order disclosure, production estimates and cancellation rights are shown before payment, never after. Prices include the tax treatment stated at checkout for the destination.

Privacy

Account, order and device registration data are collected to fulfil orders and warranty. Cloud and save data practices, child account rules and XR data handling are detailed in the full policy at launch; a data protection impact assessment is required before the VR headset launches. Data is exportable by the owner of the account.

Shipping

Devices ship direct from the manufacturer or fulfilment partner, with tracking issued from carrier evidence. Duties and taxes treatment is stated per destination at checkout; no duty rate is hard coded into pricing, and a customs broker ruling governs at order time.

Warranty

Standard one year limited warranty in the United States draft, with market floors applied where law requires more. Claims run by serial number through the support workflow. Warranty reserves are carried in unit economics.

Returns and refunds

Thirty day return window from delivery in the draft, market checked before launch. Refunds go to the original payment method and are marked complete only on processor confirmation.
