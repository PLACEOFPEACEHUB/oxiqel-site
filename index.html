01 The product line

Six devices. One library.

Six devices, one account, one library. Every device below is in development.

01 OXIQEL ONEHome Console A compact living room console built on a catalogue AMD APU, positioned honestly as a living room PC class machine for indie, back catalogue, esports and cloud play. In development→ 02 OXIQEL GOHandheld An OLED handheld designed around the Ryzen Z series, dockable to a television, with native and cloud play on the same account as the console. In development→ 03 OXIQEL CASTCloud Device A small streaming client for the television. It renders nothing locally; it decodes the stream from your own console or PC at home first, and from the cloud when that service exists. In development→ 04 OXIQEL CONTROLController A wireless controller built on an established manufacturing platform, set apart by Hall effect and TMR stick modules that have no potentiometer wear or drift mechanism. In development→ 05 OXIQEL VISION AIRDisplay Glasses A wearable monitor: a private big screen that plugs into a host over USB C. It is a display, with no tracking and no virtual reality, and it will never be sold as one. In development→ 06 OXIQEL VISIONVR Headset A standalone VR and MR headset planned on a published reference design, sequenced last in the line, after the account and store exist to support it. 02 The OXIQEL standard

Three rules the line never breaks

Three rules the product line never breaks.

RULE / 01

Targets are labelled targets

Every frame rate, resolution and battery figure in our engineering record is marked as a design target, not achieved and not tested, until a real prototype measures it. Marketing never quotes a target as a result.

RULE / 02

Quotes before prices

No price appears on this site until a written manufacturer quotation supports it. Cost models built from public prices are planning estimates, and they are labelled that way wherever they are used.

RULE / 03

Evidence before status

Orders, shipping and refunds in the OXIQEL DIRECT design move only on evidence: a payment confirmed by the processor, a shipment confirmed by the carrier, a refund confirmed on completion. Nothing is marked done on a promise.
